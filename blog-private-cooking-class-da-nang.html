What is a Private Cooking Class in Da Nang?

A private cooking class in Da Nang is an exclusive, hands-on culinary experience reserved just for your group. Unlike a shared group session with strangers, a private class means the kitchen, the hosts, and the entire programme are dedicated entirely to you.

At Family Kitchen Da Nang, this means cooking in our actual home — not a commercial kitchen, not a tourist school — with Jérôme and Julia, a Franco-Vietnamese couple of former professional chefs. You arrive as guests. You leave as family.

"A private cooking class is not just a lesson. It's your own Vietnamese kitchen story."

What's Included in Your Private Cooking Class

From the moment you arrive to the last bite of dessert, every detail of your private cooking class in Da Nang is taken care of. Here is what a typical session looks like.

1

☕ Welcome with Vietnamese Egg Coffee

You arrive at our home and are welcomed with a traditional cà phê trứng — Vietnam's iconic egg coffee. Rich, creamy, and utterly unique. The perfect introduction to what lies ahead.

2

🛒 Private Market Tour

Together we head to the local neighbourhood market to hand-pick the freshest herbs, vegetables and proteins for the day. This is not a tourist market — it's where locals shop every morning. A window into real Da Nang life.

3

👨‍🍳 Hands-On Cooking of 6 Dishes

Back in the kitchen, you cook six traditional Vietnamese dishes from scratch. Jérôme and Julia guide you step by step, sharing techniques, stories and the secrets behind each recipe. Everyone participates — no watching from the sidelines.

4

🍽️ Sit Down and Eat Together

You cooked it, now you eat it. Sit around the family table and enjoy everything you made, together with your hosts. It's relaxed, convivial, and genuinely one of the warmest meals you'll have in Vietnam.

5

📖 Recipe Book to Take Home

Leave with a printed booklet of all the recipes you cooked, so you can recreate the flavours of Da Nang in your own kitchen when you get home.

Vegetarian & Vegan Private Classes

Following a plant-based diet? No problem. Our private Vietnamese cooking class can be fully adapted to vegetarian or vegan requirements. Just let us know when you book and we will redesign the menu around the incredible variety of Vietnamese plant-based cooking — from tofu pho to vegan bánh xèo with mushrooms and vegetables.

Vietnamese cuisine is naturally rich in vegetables, herbs and tofu, making it one of the most naturally vegan-friendly cuisines in Asia. A private class means we have the flexibility to tailor every dish to your needs.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a private cooking class in Da Nang cost?

Our private cooking class starts from $35 USD per person. The price is the same whether you book privately or join a shared session — the private experience comes at no extra cost. Everything is included: market visit, all ingredients, 6 dishes and the recipe booklet.

What is included in a private cooking class in Da Nang?

Your private class includes: a welcome Vietnamese egg coffee, a guided local market tour, hands-on cooking of 6 traditional Vietnamese dishes with Jérôme and Julia, a full sit-down meal of everything you cooked, and a printed recipe booklet to take home. Classes run 9:30am–12:30pm or 2pm–5pm.

Can I customize the menu for my private cooking class?

Yes — menu customization is one of the key advantages of a private class. We can adapt fully for vegetarians, vegans, allergies, or personal preferences. Just mention it when you book.

How many people can join a private cooking class in Da Nang?

Between 1 and 8 people. We keep groups small to guarantee a genuinely hands-on, personal experience where everyone is actively cooking — not just watching.

Is a private cooking class suitable for complete beginners?

Absolutely. Jérôme and Julia adapt their teaching to every level. You do not need any cooking experience — just curiosity and appetite.

Do you offer private cooking classes for couples in Da Nang?

Yes! A private class for two is one of the most romantic experiences in Da Nang. Many couples book it during their honeymoon or as a special date. You cook together, eat together, and leave with a shared memory and a set of recipes.
